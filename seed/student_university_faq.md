## 1. Eligibility & Academic Domain Verification (`EDU-FAQ-01`)
Full-time students, PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers, and faculty members at accredited higher-education institutions qualify for the **Academic & Research Program**.
- **Instant Auto-Verification:** Register or update your workspace billing email using an official institutional domain, including `.edu` (US/Global), `.edu.pl` / `.ac.uk` (Europe), `.edu.tr` (Turkey), or any domain listed in the WHED/SheerID academic registry.
- **Manual Verification (`EDU-FAQ-02`):** If your university issues student emails on a standard `.pl`, `.de`, or `.org` domain without an `.edu` prefix, upload a photo of your valid **Student ID Card (Legitymacja Studencka / Öğrenci Kimlik Kartı)** or current semester enrollment certificate via **Billing -> Academic Verification**. Manual reviews complete within **24 business hours**.

## 2. Academic Discount & Free Compute Credits (`EDU-FAQ-03`)
Verified academic accounts receive:
- **80% Academic Discount** on the **Pro Plan** (reducing the cost from $49.00/month to **$9.80/month**).
- **$100.00 USD in annual LLM & Vector Compute Credits** for thesis research, capstone engineering projects, and non-commercial portfolio demonstrations.
- **Zero-Cost Classroom Sandbox:** University instructors teaching AI Engineering, NLP, or Distributed Systems courses can request a **Classroom License** granting up to **150 free student seats** for the duration of the semester.

## 3. Retroactive Refund for Students Charged Full Price (`EDU-FAQ-04`)
If a student accidentally subscribed at the standard commercial rate ($49.00/month) before applying their academic status:
- Once `.edu` / `.edu.pl` / `.edu.tr` verification is confirmed, support agents are authorized to **refund the 80% price difference ($39.20 USD)** for any charge incurred within the **last 30 days** and convert the subscription to the `$9.80/month` Academic Pro rate immediately.

## 4. Graduation Transition & Grace Period (`EDU-FAQ-05`)
Academic discounts remain valid for **12 months** per verification cycle. Upon graduation, alumni receive a **6-month Startup & Career Transition Grace Period** at **50% off** the Pro tier while preserving all existing vector collections, fine-tuned prompts, and webhook integrations.
