# Billing, Subscriptions & Refund Policy (`POL-REF-2026`)

**Document ID:** `POL-REF-2026`  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Finance & Customer Operations  

---

## 1. Standard 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee (`POL-REF-2026#SEC-1.1`)
All new self-serve subscriptions (Starter, Pro, and Team tiers) are eligible for a **100% full refund within 14 calendar days** of the initial purchase date, provided total platform usage has not exceeded **5,000 API tokens or 50 automated ticket dispatches** during the trial window.
- **Processing Time:** Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Stripe / Corporate Card / SEPA) within **3–5 business days**.
- **First-Time Renewal Grace Period (`POL-REF-2026#SEC-1.2`):** If an annual or monthly subscription auto-renews unintentionally, customers may request a full refund within **72 hours of the renewal charge** if zero billable actions occurred after the renewal timestamp.

## 2. Duplicate Charges & Billing Errors (`POL-REF-2026#SEC-2.1`)
In the event of a duplicate charge, double invoice, or gateway timeout error resulting in multiple debits:
- **Immediate Resolution:** Duplicate charges qualify for an **immediate 100% refund** regardless of account tier or usage history.
- **Compensation Credit:** Customers impacted by verified duplicate billing receive an automatic **$25.00 USD service credit** applied to their next billing cycle as a goodwill gesture.

## 3. Enterprise SLA Downtime Credits (`POL-REF-2026#SEC-3.1`)
Enterprise plans operate under a **99.95% monthly uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA)**. If monthly uptime falls below this threshold:
- **99.0% – 99.94% Uptime:** Eligible for a **10% service credit** of the monthly recurring charge (MRC).
- **95.0% – 98.99% Uptime:** Eligible for a **25% service credit** of the MRC.
- **Below 95.0% Uptime:** Eligible for a **50% service credit** or penalty-free contract termination with a prorated refund of unused prepaid annual fees.

## 4. Non-Refundable Items & Escalation Rules (`POL-REF-2026#SEC-4.1`)
- **Non-Refundable Services:** Custom on-premise deployment fees, dedicated solutions engineering hours, and third-party telecommunications pass-through costs are non-refundable once delivered.
- **Mandatory Human Escalation (`POL-REF-2026#SEC-4.2`):** Any refund request exceeding **$500.00 USD**, involving a legal dispute/chargeback threat, or requesting an exception outside the 14-day window MUST be routed to a **Senior Billing Specialist** via the Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Approval Queue before dispatch.
